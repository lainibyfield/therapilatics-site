The Practice

Not a class.
Not a clinic.
Something else.

Most people who arrive here are already active. They go to the gym. They are not deconditioned. They have a back problem, a neck problem, a hip that has not been right since the surgery two years ago — and the workouts they are doing are not fixing it and may be making it worse.

What they have not tried is work that is calibrated to their specific body, in a private room, with an instructor who came from engineering and approaches movement the way an engineer approaches a structural problem.
