—

You are active and fit, but something in your back, neck, or hip has not been right in months.

—

Physical therapy helped, then plateaued, and you are not all the way back to yourself.

—

You have been told to stay active, but the gym and group classes seem to make it worse.

—

You are managing a diagnosis and want someone who works with you one-on-one, not a room full of people.

—

You want to protect your golf or tennis game, not give it up because of your back.

The work adapts — to injury, to recovery, to a golf swing, to age.

The Work

It's a workout.
It's a class of one.
It's a corrective way of moving.

You are probably already active. You go to the gym. You are not deconditioned. But you have a back problem, a neck problem, a hip that has not been right since the surgery two years ago — and the workouts you are doing are not fixing it, and may be making it worse.

What you have not tried is work calibrated to your specific body, in a private room, with an instructor who came from engineering and looks at movement the way an engineer looks at a structural problem.

The Difference

Movement First

I do not teach everyone the same workout. I assess how you move, identify what is limiting you, and build the work around your goals — walking farther, returning to your sport, reducing pain, or getting up from the floor with confidence.

You are not buying a class. You are buying thoughtful, individualized movement work — from someone trained to the highest level of the classical method, on the original apparatus, with no one else in the room.
