What I Offer

Services

Every service is one-on-one, in a private room, on original Gratz apparatus. There are no group options and no shared equipment. That is the standard here.

How It Works
01
Start with an Assessment
One hour. I look at how your body is moving, take your history, and identify what is limiting you. New clients only.
02
A Program Built From Your Assessment
You will most likely begin with a structured program, typically six to eight weeks, built from what your assessment reveals. The plan is yours — its length and focus depend on your body, not a fixed prescription.

Ongoing Maintenance
Once the foundation is established, ongoing 45-minute maintenance sessions hold what you have built. A reserved weekly slot keeps the work consistent.
