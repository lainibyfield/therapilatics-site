Step 01

Book an Assessment

New clients start here. Select Introductory Assessment in the scheduler below. One hour. We establish what your body is doing and what the work should look like.

Step 02

Complete Your Foundation

Following your assessment, most clients begin the Foundation Period — five sessions over three weeks. Frequency accelerates learning. This is where the work starts.

Step 03

Establish Your Practice

Ongoing clients book private sessions or hold a weekly slot through the Monthly Retainer. Consistency is where results compound. The slot is yours as long as the retainer is active.

Scheduling Policy

Appointment Only

There is no walk-in availability at either location. All sessions are booked in advance through Setmore. New clients begin with an Introductory Assessment — this is not optional and cannot be skipped. Hands-on work begins at the Foundation Period.

Cancellation Policy

24-Hour Notice Required

All services are prepaid. Late arrivals receive the remainder of their scheduled hour. Cancellations with more than 24 hours notice may be rescheduled within the same calendar week, subject to availability. Cancellations within 24 hours forfeit the session.
