Scheduling Policy

Appointment Only

There is no walk-in availability at either location. All sessions are booked in advance. New clients begin with an Introductory Assessment. Assessments are by appointment, including Sunday afternoons — if you do not see a time that works, request one above.

Cancellation Policy

24-Hour Notice Required

All services are prepaid. Late arrivals receive the remainder of their scheduled hour. Cancellations with more than 24 hours notice may be rescheduled within 7 days of the original session, subject to availability. Cancellations within 24 hours forfeit the session.
