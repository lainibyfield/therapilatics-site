The Workout

Yes, there will be Teasers.

Expect an approachable Pilates class, the infamous towel workout, and progressive exercises that build toward one of the method's best-known movements: the Teaser.

You do not have to be able to do a Teaser to come. That is rather the point.

What to bring

  • Your own mat — a thicker mat is recommended.
  • Comfortable clothes you can move in.
  • A little curiosity. Experience is not required.

Water will be provided. Coffee will be available at select sessions.

Why Teasers for Tatas?

Movement with a larger purpose.

Teasers for Tatas is a 501(c)(3) organization created to celebrate and support the breast cancer community through Pilates, wellness, and connection.

Learn more

Visit the official Teasers for Tatas website or read about Eve Gentry, a first-generation Pilates teacher whose breast-cancer recovery became part of Pilates history.

Radiance & Resilience

A smaller, more intimate experience centered specifically on exercise for older adults.

Laini will lead a short movement experience focused on how exercise changes as we age — including strength, balance, mobility, recovery, and maintaining independence — as part of her Certified Geriatric Care Professional certification.

The evening is expected to include

  • Light hors d'oeuvres
  • A short guided Pilates experience
  • A Pilates mat to take home
  • Salon tour and refreshments
  • A silent auction benefiting Teasers for Tatas
